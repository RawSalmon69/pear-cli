2. Runner frame cadence
-----------------------

  RunCat (menubar_runcat) — https://github.com/Kyome22/menubar_runcat
    — Apache-2.0, Copyright Takuto Nakamura (Kyome22).

  Pear's CPU-load-to-frame-interval mapping is adopted from this project's
  AppDelegate, which computes:

      interval = 0.2 / max(1, min(20, cpuPercent / 5))

  MODIFICATIONS: Pear changed the lower bound of that interval from 10 ms
  (upstream's clamp of speed at 20) to 50 ms. Upstream swaps `button.image`
  on a bare NSStatusItem; Pear's frame swap re-renders a SwiftUI menu-bar
  label, which is far more expensive per tick. At 10 ms the redraw churn
  alone held the app near 28% CPU on a loaded machine, and that burn fed
  the very load signal that sets the cadence, so the sprint sustained
  itself. The idle endpoint (0.2 s), the divisor (5) and the clamp shape
  are unchanged. See RunnerCadence in Tools/RunCat/RunnerModel.swift.
