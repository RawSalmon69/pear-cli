Refund policy

Fourteen days. No interrogation.

If Pear isn't for you, we'll refund it. You don't have to explain why, and we won't try to talk you out of it.

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Try before you buy

Pear runs in full for 14 days before you pay anything — no card, no account. We'd rather you found out during the trial than asked for your money back, so please use it.

The policy

  • You can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase.
  • No reason required.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method.
  • Your licence stops being valid once refunded, and you should delete the app.

After 14 days we'll still look at it. If Pear is broken for you, if it doesn't do what this website said, or if something went wrong with your order, write to us — a rule shouldn't stop us fixing a genuine problem.

How to ask

Either:

  • Email contact@phanthawas.dev with the email address you bought with, or
  • Use the link in the receipt Paddle sent you.

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, which is the Merchant of Record for all orders and processes refunds. Once approved, the money typically appears within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

The one exception

We may decline where a refund is clearly being used to keep using the software for free — for example repeatedly buying and refunding the same product. That's it.

Your statutory rights

This policy sits on top of, and never replaces, the rights you have as a consumer where you live — including the EU and UK right of withdrawal for digital purchases.
