Privacy policy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

Not a slogan. Pear contains no analytics library, no crash reporter, no accounts, and no server of ours to talk to. This page lists every exception, and there are two.

Last updated: 31 July 2026

What Pear stores, and where

All of it stays in your own user folder, readable by you and deletable at any time:

  • Screenshots — saved to the folder you choose. Captures you haven't saved are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/PearCompanion/Captures for seven days, then removed.
  • Recognised text, QR contents, colour swatches — produced on device by Apple's Vision framework and kept only as long as the preview card that owns them.
  • Clipboard history, scratchpad notes, shelf items — stored under ~/Library/Application Support/PearCompanion.
  • Settings — standard macOS preferences for this app.
  • Your licence — a signed file. Verified locally. It contains the email address you bought with.

Pear does not read, index or transmit any of it. There is nowhere for it to go.

The only two network connections

  1. Update checks. Pear asks GitHub whether a newer version exists. GitHub sees your IP address and the request, as any website would. No identifier of ours is attached.
  2. The optional high-quality background-removal model. Only if you switch on High-quality mode does Pear download a ~195 MB machine-learning model from our GitHub release page. It then runs entirely on your Mac, forever offline. If you never turn that on, this never happens.

That is the complete list. Your licence is not checked over the network — there is no activation server, so Pear keeps working with no internet at all.

System permissions

macOS will ask you for some of these. Each is used locally, for the tool that needs it, and nothing captured is transmitted:

  • Screen Recording — to take screenshots and show window previews.
  • Accessibility — to move windows, read menu shortcuts, and arrange menu-bar items.
  • Files and folders — to save screenshots where you asked and to show disk usage.
  • Notifications — to tell you a capture or scan finished. Since version 2.15, notifications never quote the contents of what was scanned.

Buying Pear

Purchases are handled by Paddle.com, which is the Merchant of Record and processes payment on our behalf. Paddle receives your name, email address, billing country and payment details under their privacy policy. We never see or store your card details.

From Paddle we receive your email address, order reference and country, so we can issue your licence and honour refunds and support. We keep that for as long as we are required to for accounting, and we do not sell it, rent it, or send you marketing you didn't ask for.

If you email support, we have your email and whatever you tell us. That's it.

Your rights

Because Pear collects nothing, most data requests concern only your purchase record held by us and by Paddle. Write to contact@phanthawas.dev and we will confirm, correct, export or delete what we hold, subject to accounting obligations. If you are in the EEA or UK, that includes your rights under the GDPR; if you are in California, your rights under the CCPA.

Pear is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app's release notes will say so. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
