Questions

How many Macs can I use one licence on?

Any Mac you own or personally use. One licence per person, not per machine. Your licence carries your email address, so please do not pass it on.

Does Pear need an internet connection?

No. Licences are signed files verified on your Mac. There is no activation server to reach.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within fourteen days, without giving a reason. See the refund policy.

What about the cleanup, disk and stats tools?

Those come from pear, a separate command-line tool that is free and open source under the GPL-3.0. Install it and those tiles work. Without it, the rest of the app is unaffected.

What data leaves my Mac?

Nothing from your work. The app has no analytics and no server of ours to reach. See the privacy policy.
