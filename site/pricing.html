Questions people actually ask

How many Macs can I use it on?

Any Mac you personally own or use. One licence per person, not per machine. Please don't hand your licence to other people — it carries your email address.

Does it phone home?

No. Your licence is a signed file the app verifies on your Mac. There is no activation server, so Pear works on a plane, behind a firewall, and in ten years when this website is gone. See Privacy.

What if I don't like it?

14 days to change your mind, no reason needed. See Refunds.

What about the Mac cleanup features?

Cleanup, disk analysis and system stats come from the pear command-line tool, which is free, open source (GPL-3.0), and completely separate from this app. Install it and those tiles light up. Don't, and the rest of Pear works exactly the same.

Do you get my screenshots, my clipboard, or my text?

No. None of it leaves your Mac. That isn't a policy choice we could quietly reverse — the app has no analytics and no server to send it to.
